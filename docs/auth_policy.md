# Account Login and Password Reset Policy

## Login
Users sign in with their registered email address and password. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all accounts created after January 2024. Supported MFA methods are authenticator apps (TOTP) and hardware security keys. SMS codes are not supported as an MFA method.

## Failed login attempts
After 5 consecutive failed login attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes. A locked account can be unlocked early only by completing a password reset.

## Password reset
Users can request up to 3 password reset emails per hour. Each reset link expires 15 minutes after it is sent and can be used only once. After a successful reset, all active sessions on other devices are signed out.

## Password requirements
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and must not match any of the user's last 5 passwords.

## Sessions
Web sessions expire after 8 hours of inactivity. Mobile app sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity.

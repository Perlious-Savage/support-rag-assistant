# Payment Processing and Withdrawal Timing

## Eligibility
Only KYC-verified users can request a withdrawal. The minimum withdrawal amount is 20 USD.

## Processing times
- Bank transfer (ACH): 1 to 3 business days.
- SEPA transfer: 1 business day.
- Card refund: up to 5 business days.
Withdrawals requested after 16:00 UTC are processed on the next business day.

## Daily limits
Verified users can withdraw up to 10,000 USD per day. Larger withdrawals require approval from the Finance team.

## Pending and failed withdrawals
A withdrawal remains pending while fraud checks run. If a withdrawal fails, the funds are returned to the user's balance within 24 hours and the user is notified by email.

# Incident Escalation Policy

## Severity levels
- SEV1: full outage or data breach affecting customers. Page the on-call engineer immediately and notify the Head of Engineering within 15 minutes.
- SEV2: major feature degraded for many customers. The on-call engineer must acknowledge within 30 minutes.
- SEV3: minor issue with a workaround available. Handled during business hours through the normal ticket queue.

## Communication
For SEV1 incidents, the status page must be updated within 30 minutes of the incident being declared and then at least every hour until resolution.

## Post-incident review
A written post-incident review is required for every SEV1 and SEV2 incident within 5 business days of resolution.

## Escalation path
Support agents escalate to the on-call engineer through the paging tool. If the on-call engineer does not acknowledge within the required time, the page escalates automatically to the secondary on-call.

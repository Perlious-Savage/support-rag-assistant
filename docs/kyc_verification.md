# KYC / Identity Verification Rules

## When verification is required
Identity verification (KYC) must be completed before a user can withdraw funds or deposit more than 1,000 USD in total.

## Accepted documents
Accepted documents are a valid passport, a national ID card or a driving licence. Documents must not be expired. A selfie liveness check is required together with the document upload.

## Review time
Automated checks usually complete within 10 minutes. Cases flagged for manual review are processed within 2 business days.

## Rejections and retries
If verification is rejected, the user may resubmit up to 3 times within 30 days. After 3 failed attempts, the case is escalated to the Compliance team and the user cannot resubmit until Compliance responds.

## Data retention
Verification documents are retained for 5 years after the account is closed, as required by anti-money-laundering regulations.
